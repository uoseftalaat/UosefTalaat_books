# Surface Brief: index.html

## Scope and Mode

Scope: Main portfolio and reading showcase surface (`index.html`).
Visitor Mode: Experience. The visitor is inside Uosef's personal literary atelier; the interface recedes to let the books, typography, and personal reflections lead.

## Audience and Purpose

Audience: Discerning readers, thinkers, book club members, and followers of Uosef Talaat.
Job: Discover thoughtful book recommendations, observe a genuine 217-book reading journey, and engage with Uosef's candid literary commentary.
Memorable Moment: The dramatic, tactile 3D perspective slider for the top 5-star books set against a deep charcoal midnight backdrop, paired with the cinematic film-strip hero portrait in bespoke Arabic typography.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An authentic Arabic literary atelier that rejects sterile SaaS portfolio templates and AI-generated pastel cookie-cutter cards in favor of a tactile, high-contrast, deeply human editorial experience inspired by Haruki Murakami's dramatic book exhibitions and classical Arabic typography.

OWN-WORLD: Ground of warm Japanese paper (#F5F1EB) juxtaposed with ink charcoal (#1C1A17) and lacquer cinnabar red (#C0392B). Monumental Reem Kufi display typography, literary Amiri serif for titles and quotes, and crisp Tajawal for interface text. Tactile book covers with physical depth, soft directional ambient shadows, and realistic spine highlights.

STORY: The visitor enters through Uosef's candid, first-person voice. They discover who he is through his authentic curiosity, see what he is immersed in reading right now, interact with his handpicked 5-star masterworks in three dimensions, explore his curated video reflections, and dive into a comprehensive archive of 217 books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cinematic composition featuring Uosef's monochrome/vintage film-strip portrait integrated into a deep atmospheric gradient. The monumental headline "يوسف طلعت" in bold Reem Kufi anchors the upper right, accompanied by the personal motto "قارئ · مفكر · مستكشف بين السطور", an elegant minimalist floating navigation bar, and an understated scroll prompt pointing toward the literary journey.

FORM: The Literary Atelier & Book Archive, Position 1 on the confirmed design system list. seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
