# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5 / Vanilla CSS3 / Modern JavaScript (ES6+), deployed to GitHub Pages with automated monthly GitHub Actions data sync.

## Users

Readers, book enthusiasts, thinkers, and followers of Uosef Talaat looking for authentic book recommendations, reading journeys, and intellectual reflections across history, philosophy, literature, and psychology.

## Product Purpose

A personal, high-craft editorial reading website for Uosef Talaat. It showcases his reading life (217 books read and counting), current reads, top 5-star literature, and YouTube thoughts in an immersive, design-first digital atelier.

## Positioning

An authorial, authentic digital sanctuary for a true book lover. It explicitly rejects generic cookie-cutter portfolio templates and corporate SaaS aesthetics in favor of a tactile, high-contrast literary editorial experience (inspired by Haruki Murakami's editorial aesthetics and classical book design).

## Operating Context

- Arabic language (RTL native layout throughout).
- Viewed on desktop (spacious literary layout, 3D perspective slider) and mobile/tablet (fluid responsive typography, touch-enabled book exploration).
- Data refreshed on the 1st of every month automatically via GitHub Actions from Goodreads RSS.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Design First**: Visual craft, typography hierarchy, tactile depth, and editorial atmosphere outrank superficial feature bloat.
- **Full Arabic RTL**: Flawless right-to-left layout, correct punctuation, Arabic numerals/formatting, with clean handling of bilingual book titles.
- **Real Goodreads Pipeline**: Seamless parsing of Goodreads RSS for all 217 books, currently-reading shelf, and top 5-star rated books.
- **Curated Media**: Hardcoded featured YouTube videos and high-fidelity local assets.
- **Zero Framework Bloat**: Pure native web standards for instant loading, zero runtime dependencies, and effortless GitHub Pages hosting.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone of Voice**: First-person Arabic, direct, humble, intensely curious:
  > «بقرأ كتير. مش لأني ذكي — لأني فضولي. ٢١٧ كتاب في التاريخ والفكر والأدب والجريمة، وكل كتاب علّمني إن في أكتر مما أعرفه. بشارككم اللي بقرأه على يوتيوب وإنستجرام — بعيون حد بيتعلم، مش حد بيحاضر.»
- **Visual Identity**:
  - Warm paper tone (`#F5F1EB`), ink charcoal (`#1C1A17`), intense lacquer red (`#C0392B`), and antique gold accents.
  - Typography: **Reem Kufi** (monumental editorial display headlines), **Amiri** (literary serif, book titles, and philosophical quotes), and **Tajawal** (crystal-clear modern body and UI).
  - Authentic hero imagery using Uosef's film-strip profile portrait.

## Product Principles

1. **Design is the Soul**: Every pixel, margin, letterform, and shadow must feel intentional and crafted by a human art director.
2. **Tactility over Flatness**: Books are physical objects; their covers have depth, weight, lighting, and dimensional presence.
3. **Restraint and Focus**: No gratuitous animations or AI gimmicks. Editorial whitespace, confident typographic scale, and literary dignity.
4. **Honest Voice**: Personal, conversational Arabic without posturing or academic pretension.
